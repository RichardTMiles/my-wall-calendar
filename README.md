# 📅 How to Find Your iCal (.ics) URL

To connect your calendar to apps like our wall-mounted display, you'll need the iCal URL (also called `.ics` or "calendar feed" URL) from your calendar service.

Most services require you to **publish** or **share** the calendar before providing a link. These links typically start with `webcal://` or `https://` and end in `.ics`.

---

## 🔗 Google Calendar

1. Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) on desktop.
2. Click the **gear icon → Settings**.
3. In the left sidebar under **"Settings for my calendars"**, select the calendar.
4. Scroll to the **Integrate calendar** section.
5. Copy the **"Secret address in iCal format"**.

> ✅ Use the **secret** address — it includes private events.
> 🔒 Treat this URL like a password.

---

## 🍎 iCloud Calendar

1. Go to [icloud.com/calendar](https://icloud.com/calendar) and sign in.
2. Click the 📷 icon next to your calendar name.
3. Check **"Public Calendar"**.
4. Copy the provided **webcal://** link.

> ⚠️ Anyone with the link can view the calendar.

---

## 🧭 Outlook / Microsoft 365

1. Go to [Outlook on the web](https://outlook.office.com/calendar/).
2. Click the **gear icon → View all Outlook settings**.
3. Go to **Calendar → Shared calendars**.
4. Under **Publish a calendar**, select your calendar and permission level.
5. Click **Publish**, then copy the **ICS link**.

> 👥 Choose **Can view all details** for full event info.

---

## 📘 Facebook Events

1. Go to any Facebook **Page** or **Group** with events.
2. Click the **Events** tab.
3. Click **Add to Calendar**.
4. Right-click the button and choose **Copy link address**.

> 📝 Note: Facebook calendar feeds only include upcoming public events.

---

## 🛠 General Tips

- Most iCal URLs end in `.ics` and may use `webcal://`, `http://`, or `https://`.
- If you're not seeing an iCal link, look for a "share", "publish", or "integrate" option.
- If all else fails, search for "[your calendar app] export iCal" in Google.
